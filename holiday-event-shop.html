Holiday Event Shop FAQs

What should I wear to a Christmas party in 2026?

Sequins are the season's default for evening parties — a fully lined sequin cocktail or midi dress is the one-buy answer. For daytime and family parties, a knit sweater dress in a festive colour (green, red, burgundy) photographs just as well and is considerably more comfortable.

When should I order a Thanksgiving or New Year's Eve outfit?

For Thanksgiving (Nov 26), order by mid-November so exchanges for size are still possible. For New Year's Eve, the honest deadline is mid-December: party dresses in popular colours sell through as the month goes on, and the catalogue prices on this page were checked on November 11.

Are these prices guaranteed through the events?

No — Amazon prices move, and Black Friday week moves them most. Prices here are the LOOKIQ catalogue prices checked on November 11, 2026, given so you can judge a deal when you see one; the live price is always on the product's Amazon page.
